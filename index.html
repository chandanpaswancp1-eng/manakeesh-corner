Our story

A bakery born inside a hive.

Manakeesh Corner is one of Food Hive’s live, physical brands — a focused Lebanese kitchen inside a modern multi-brand cloud-kitchen ecosystem built for the GCC.

Food Hive operates more than a hundred physical and virtual concepts across American, Italian, Arabic, healthy and dessert kitchens. While Smash & Stack owns the burger lane, Manakeesh Corner owns the dawn: zaatar oil soaking into blistered dough, cheese pulling across a wooden board, the scent of sumac and sesame.

We bake for delivery-first mornings and late-night cravings alike — centralized kitchens, tight recipes, and the same Levantine ritual that has opened Lebanese days for generations.

The oven list

Signature manakeesh

Classics first. Generous toppings. Dough pressed with the fingertips — naqasha, to engrave — then baked until the edges blister.

Zaatar

Wild thyme, toasted sesame, tangy sumac and extra-virgin olive oil on a thin, blistered crust. The original morning.

Vegetarian

Akkawi Cheese

Salty, stretchy Levantine akkawi melted into golden pools. Simple, generous, impossible to share.

Zaatar & Cheese
